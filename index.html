Graveyard Backups

A calculator reset wipes Graveyard's save. Scan the game's backup QR to keep a copy, and put it back over USB after a wipe.

Add a backup

  1. In Graveyard, choose BACKUP on the title menu. The screen shows your save as a QR code.
  2. Point your phone's camera at it and open the link. The backup is saved on this site by itself.
  3. Already here? Tap Scan the QR instead.

Hold the calculator screen inside the square.

Your backups

Backups stay in this browser on this device; nothing is sent anywhere. Save a backup file or copy its link to keep a second copy. QR reading by jsQR (Apache 2.0).
